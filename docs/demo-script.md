# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Record in English. The posko console is in English. The citizen app is Indonesian-first with English subtitles on every key action. The replay lasts about 78 seconds, so start it during the operator scene and talk over the stream.

| Time | What you show | What you say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Title card: TIPO, TIten POsko. A still of the Dayeuhkolot map. | "Simeulue turned warnings into songs. We turn reports into action. BNPB recorded 1,730 disasters in 2026. In a flood, the posko drowns in duplicate chats, old photos, and voice notes in Sundanese and Javanese. TIPO reads the signs so the operator can act first." |
| 0:20–0:50 | Android or the citizen preview. Tap *Lapor sekarang*, open *Mode demo*, tap the Sundanese sample, send it. The status timeline fills in. | "A resident sends a voice note: water at chest height, an elderly person trapped on the second floor. Intake writes a structured ticket in English and keeps the original transcript." |
| 0:50–1:30 | Posko console. Press Replay scenario. Watch the queue, the map, and the agent activity feed. Filter to *Unconfirmed* and open the red *Possible hoax* ticket, then its Evidence tab. | "Twenty-five reports arrive in just over a minute, including WhatsApp and PetaBencana. Duplicates collapse into one Dayeuhkolot incident. This photo stays out: the EXIF date is 2024, the GPS is far from Bandung, and the frame is a coastal watermark. Credibility stays low. A person still decides." |
| 1:30–2:10 | Open the critical Dayeuhkolot ticket. Point at elderly, two boats, ETA, and the Priority and Logistics tabs. Press Approve and send alert. The confirm dialog shows the alert text, the 2 km radius, and the device count. Send it. | "Eight elderly people are described as trapped. The triage agent suggests two rubber boats from Posko Dayeuhkolot and a water estimate. I check exactly what goes out and to how many phones, then I approve. The model does not send this." |
| 2:10–2:35 | Citizen inbox: report verified. Mention the three listeners inside 2 km. | "The reporter gets 'team dispatched'. People standing by nearby get a short alert with no personal data. The operator already has the critical-ticket notice." |
| 2:35–2:50 | Open the Sitrep tab. Press Copy sitrep, or open the full page. | "One click drafts a BNPB-style situation report: event, place, impact, needs, and the team. It is labeled a draft until a human approves it." |
| 2:50–3:00 | Architecture slide from docs/architecture.md. | "Gemini 3.8 Flash and ADK run on Cloud Run. BMKG is the official cross-check. Next: a real WhatsApp channel, and a BPBD pilot. TIPO. Read the signs. Act first." |

Reset the board before a second take. Replay always starts from the same seed.
